Privacy Policy for Zen Video Player

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Zen Video Player is designed to play local video and audio files and to stream media from user-provided links. This policy explains what data the app and its integrated third-party services may collect and how it is used.

Data Collection and Usage

Zen Video Player does not ask you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

However, the app and integrated third-party services may collect non-personal or device-related data, including:

  • Device or advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location derived from network information such as IP address
  • App interaction data (for example, ad views and taps)
  • Diagnostic and analytics data (for example, crash reports and basic usage events)

This data is used to provide advertising, measure ad performance, improve app stability, and support core app functionality.

Third-Party Services

This app uses third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob (advertising; may serve ads through mediation partners)
  • Meta Audience Network (advertising, via AdMob mediation)
  • Unity Ads (advertising, via AdMob mediation)
  • Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting)
  • Google Cast (optional Chromecast playback)

These services may collect and process data such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Approximate location (for example, from IP address)
  • App interaction data
  • Diagnostic information

This data may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, analytics, fraud prevention, and app performance monitoring.

We do not control how these third parties collect or use data. Please refer to their privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy

Permissions

The app may request access to device permissions only as needed to:

  • Browse and play local video and audio files
  • Stream media from a network URL you provide
  • Discover and connect to Cast devices on your local network (optional)
  • Keep the screen on or support background playback where supported by the device

Media files on your device are processed locally for playback. Zen Video Player does not upload your personal media library to our servers.

The app does not request precise GPS location permission.

Data Sharing

Some data (such as device identifiers, approximate location, and app interaction data) may be collected by third-party advertising and analytics providers and shared with their partners for advertising, analytics, and app performance purposes only.

Data Security

Data transmitted by the app and integrated third-party services is sent using secure protocols such as HTTPS/TLS where supported.

Data Deletion

Zen Video Player does not operate user accounts and does not store personal user profiles on our own servers.

Data collected by third-party advertising and analytics providers is handled under their own privacy policies and retention practices. You may be able to limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

If you have privacy questions or requests related to this app, contact us using the email below.

Children's Privacy

Zen Video Player is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
